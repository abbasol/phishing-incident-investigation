MITRE ATT&CK MAPPING
====================

Technique:
T1566.002 — Phishing: Spearphishing Link

Evidence:
- Phishing email containing a suspicious verification URL.
- URL attempts to imitate a Microsoft 365 account verification workflow.
- Sender domain is a Microsoft lookalike domain.
- SPF and DMARC authentication failed.

Assessment:
The available evidence is consistent with the use of a phishing link to direct the victim toward a credential-harvesting or fraudulent authentication page.

Confidence:
High

Limitation:
No evidence currently confirms that credentials were successfully submitted or stolen.
