INCIDENT VERDICT
================

Classification:
Phishing Attempt

Confidence:
High

Severity:
Medium

Compromise Status:
Not Confirmed

Key Evidence:
- Microsoft lookalike sender domain.
- Suspicious account-verification URL.
- SPF authentication failure.
- DKIM signature not present.
- DMARC authentication failure.
- Domain returned NXDOMAIN during DNS analysis.
- No malicious detection reported by VirusTotal at the time of analysis.

Analyst Conclusion:
The collected evidence strongly indicates a phishing attempt designed to impersonate Microsoft 365 and potentially redirect the recipient to a fraudulent authentication page.

There is currently no evidence confirming successful credential theft or endpoint compromise.
