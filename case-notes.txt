Incident: Suspected Microsoft 365 Phishing

Reported URL:
https://login-microsoft365-security.com/verify

Initial observation:
The URL claims to be related to Microsoft 365 account verification.
Finding 01 — WHOIS Analysis

Domain:
login-microsoft365-security.com

Result:
No match found in the .com WHOIS registry.

Initial assessment:
The domain does not appear to be registered in the WHOIS registry at the time of investigation.

Note:
WHOIS status alone is not sufficient to classify the URL as malicious. Additional DNS and threat-intelligence analysis is required.

Finding 02 — DNS Analysis

Domain:
login-microsoft365-security.com

Query:
A record

Result:
NXDOMAIN

Interpretation:
The DNS query returned NXDOMAIN, indicating that the domain does not currently resolve to an IPv4 address through the queried DNS infrastructure.

Assessment:
The DNS result is consistent with the WHOIS finding that no registration was found. Further investigation is required before reaching a final verdict.

Finding 03 — Threat Intelligence Analysis

Indicator:
login-microsoft365-security.com

Source:
VirusTotal

Result:
No malicious detections were identified for the queried domain.

Assessment:
No current malicious reputation was observed. However, the domain was also found to be unregistered/non-resolving during WHOIS and DNS analysis.

Conclusion:
VirusTotal results alone are insufficient to classify the phishing email as benign. Further email and header analysis is required.

Finding 04 — URL Structure Analysis

URL:
https://login-microsoft365-security.com/verify

Scheme:
HTTPS

Domain:
login-microsoft365-security.com

Path:
/verify

Assessment:
The URL uses HTTPS, but the domain does not belong to the official microsoft.com domain. The "/verify" path is consistent with an account verification theme commonly observed in phishing scenarios.

Note:
HTTPS does not indicate that a website is legitimate. Additional evidence is required for final classification.

Finding 05 — Email Authentication Analysis

Observed Results:
SPF: FAIL
DKIM: NONE
DMARC: FAIL

Sender Domain:
micros0ft365-security.com

Source IP:
203.0.113.45

Assessment:
The email failed SPF and DMARC authentication checks and did not contain a valid DKIM signature. The sender domain also uses a lookalike representation of "Microsoft" by replacing the letter "o" with the digit "0".

These indicators collectively increase the likelihood that the email is a phishing attempt.

Confidence:
High

Finding 06 — IOC DNS Correlation

The identified suspicious domains did not return A records through the DNS resolver used during the investigation.

Domains tested:
- micros0ft365-security.com
- mail.micros0ft365-security.com
- login-microsoft365-security.com

Assessment:
No active IPv4 resolution was observed for the tested domains at the time of analysis.

Note:
The absence of DNS resolution does not by itself prove that an indicator is benign or malicious.
